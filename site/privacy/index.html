Stillweb Privacy Policy

Effective 3 October 2026

In short: Stillweb does not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything Stillweb remembers stays on your device.

What stays on your device

To work as a browser, Stillweb keeps the following only on your device: open tabs, browsing history, bookmarks, your downloads list, your settings (including sites where you turned protection off) and a count of blocked ads and trackers. Websites may also store cookies and site data in the browser, as in any browser.

You can delete this at any time with Settings › Clear browsing data now, or turn on Clear data on exit to start fresh each time. Deleting the app removes everything.

Private tabs

Pages opened in private tabs are never saved to history. Their cookies and site data are deleted when you close your last private tab, and again whenever the app starts. On Android, private tabs are also hidden from screenshots and the recent-apps screen.

Connections Stillweb makes

  • Websites you visit. Pages load directly from the sites you open. Stillweb blocks known ads and trackers, but each website's own privacy policy applies to what it collects.
  • Your search engine. Searches typed in the address bar go to the search engine you choose (Google by default; you can switch to a private search engine such as DuckDuckGo in Settings). Search suggestions come only from your own bookmarks and history on the device, so nothing is sent while you type.
  • Filter list updates. About once a day Stillweb downloads updated blocking lists as plain files from our update server. Like any web request this reveals your IP address to the hosting provider; the request contains no identifiers, and we do not collect or keep anything about you.
  • Android only: the system WebView's Safe Browsing protection checks pages against a list of known dangerous sites using shortened, hashed web addresses. Android WebView usage statistics are switched off.

Signals to websites

Stillweb sends the Global Privacy Control signal with every page, telling sites you do not want your personal data sold or shared. It blocks cross-site cookies by default and upgrades connections to HTTPS when sites support it.

Permissions

  • Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint (optional): used only to unlock private tabs. The check is done by your device's operating system; Stillweb never receives biometric data.
  • Notifications (Android, optional): used only to show playback controls while music or video plays in the background. Private tabs show "Private tab" instead of what is playing.
  • Files: only the files you choose when a website asks you to upload something, and the files you choose to download.

Children

Stillweb is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
