Stillweb Plus

  • Restore a purchase: open Settings › Stillweb Plus and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same account you bought it with.
  • Cancel or change a subscription: on iPhone and iPad, Settings app › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, Google Play › Payments and subscriptions. In Stillweb, Settings › Stillweb Plus › Manage subscription opens the same page.
  • Refunds are handled by the store you bought from (Apple or Google Play).
  • Ad blocking always stays free; Plus adds extra features.

Common questions

  • A site doesn't work properly. Tap the shield in the address bar and turn protection off for that site. It's remembered for that site only.
  • Music or video stops when I leave the app. Playing in the background is a Stillweb Plus feature: Settings › Play in background.
  • I forgot to keep a private tab. Private tabs are never saved; their data is deleted when they close, by design.
  • Blocking lists update automatically every day; no action is needed.
