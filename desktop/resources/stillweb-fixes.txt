! Stillweb's own fixes, for every app (phones and desktop): sites where the shared lists fall short.
! Same syntax as the other lists. Keep each rule next to the address it was tested on and
! the date, and remove it once the shared lists cover the site again.
!
! The "ev" video player (hdstream4u.com and hosts like it). Its ad module opens other sites
! on clicks after a minute of watching and draws a banner over the video. The shared lists
! block it under its old name (static.js). Tested on https://hdstream4u.com/file/0w4zg7o8rp7a, 2026-10-10.
/assets/jquery/ev-static.js$script
!
! LookMovie: its pop-under and redirect scripts (ad zone 130116) load from addresses that
! change every few hours, so they are kept out by type: no other site's scripts, except the
! two it needs (Google's reCAPTCHA, and cdnjs). Its player is its own. Tested 2026-11-01 on
! https://www.lookmovie2.to/movies/play/37961714-surviving-hurricane-otis-2026
*$script,3p,domain=lookmovie2.*|lookmovie.*
@@||google.com^$script,3p,domain=lookmovie2.*|lookmovie.*
@@||gstatic.com^$script,3p,domain=lookmovie2.*|lookmovie.*
@@||cdnjs.cloudflare.com^$script,3p,domain=lookmovie2.*|lookmovie.*
! Without its ad, the player waits behind a "Premium membership?" screen until a countdown
! (`tortureTimeout` in /api/v1/security/movie-access) has run out and it is closed by hand.
! The countdown is set to 0, as uBlock Origin's Annoyances list does (that list is only on
! with Calm Mode here); the player then starts straight away.
lookmovie2.*,lookmovie.*##+js(trusted-replace-xhr-response, /Timeout":\d+/, Timeout":0, /api/v)
